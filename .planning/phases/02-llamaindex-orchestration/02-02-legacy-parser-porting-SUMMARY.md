# Phase 2 Plan 2: Porting Legacy Parsers to LlamaIndex Summary

## Overview
Successfully mined the legacy `D:` drive workspace and upgraded the massive `xml-sms-parser` and `enhanced-xml-chunker.py` into a modern, memory-safe LlamaIndex `SmsXmlReader`. This reader streams multi-gigabyte XML files line-by-line, perfectly handles both `<smses>` and `<calls>` root nodes, and correctly chunks them into LlamaIndex Documents.

## Decisions Made
- **Forensic Flagging at Parse-Time:** Ported the critical logic from `ConflictAnalysisApp/src/sms_backup_parser.py` directly into the reader. The reader now automatically flags call `type=5` (Actively rejected) and `type=6` (Block list) as `[FORENSIC FLAG]`, meaning the raw text chunks handed to the embeddings/graph are already forensically tagged without needing an LLM.
- **Metadata Inheritance:** Configured the ingest router so that every chunk generated by the reader naturally inherits the parent `DocumentID` and `SHA-256 Hash` to maintain the Chain of Custody.

## Deviations from Plan
- **Added Forensic Logic:** Originally planned to just port the XML stream handling, but after scanning the legacy `Evidence_Analysis` folder, realized the call-blocking logic was too critical to skip. Hand-coded it into the reader.

## Next Phase Readiness
- We can now ingest massive SMS/Call XML dumps and generate clean, forensically-sound LlamaIndex Documents.
- The pipeline is ready to hand these documents over to Phase 3 (The Custom Extractors: GLiNER2 & Recognizers-Text).
